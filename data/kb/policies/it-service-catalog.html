Request types

Requests are raised in the Service Desk portal at https://help.halcyon-dynamics.example. Each request type has a standard fulfilment time:

  • Access request to an application or shared drive: 2 business days after approval.
  • New standard laptop: 5 business days after approval.
  • New software (not in catalogue): up to 10 business days for security review.
  • Shared mailbox creation: 3 business days.
  • New starter setup: requires 10 business days notice.
