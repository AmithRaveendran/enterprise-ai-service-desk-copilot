Requesting Access to a Shared Drive

Departmental shared drives live on \\files.halcyon-dynamics.example. Each folder has a named data owner (usually the department head) who approves access.

How to request access

  1. Open the Service Desk portal and select Access > Shared Drive Access.
  2. Enter the full path of the folder, for example \\files.halcyon-dynamics.example\Finance\Reports.
  3. Choose Read or Read/Write access.
  4. Give a business reason. Requests without a reason are returned.
  5. The data owner approves or rejects the request. Access is granted within 2 business days of approval.

Mapping the drive

On Windows, open File Explorer, right-click This PC, choose Map network drive, pick a letter and enter the folder path. Tick Reconnect at sign-in. On macOS, use Finder > Go > Connect to Server and enter smb://files.halcyon-dynamics.example/Finance.

You must be on the corporate network or connected to the VPN. Shared drives are not available on Halcyon-BYOD or Halcyon-Guest Wi-Fi.

Restricted folders

Folders containing Confidential or Restricted data (see the Information Security Policy, POL-0005) need extra approval from the Security team, and access is reviewed every 90 days. Access that is not re-confirmed is removed automatically.

"Access denied" after approval

  • Sign out of Windows and back in so your new group membership is applied.
  • Disconnect and remap the drive.
  • If it still fails after 4 hours, reply to your request ticket.
