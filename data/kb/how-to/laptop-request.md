# Requesting a New or Replacement Laptop

All laptops are owned and managed by Halcyon Dynamics. Laptops are requested through the
Service Desk portal; please do not purchase hardware on an expense claim.

## Standard models

| Profile | Model | Typical users |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Lenovo ThinkPad T14 (16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD) | Most office and remote staff |
| Power | Lenovo ThinkPad P16s (32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD) | Engineering, data and design teams |
| Mac | Apple MacBook Pro 14" (M-series, 18 GB RAM) | Design and software engineering, with manager justification |

Non-standard hardware requires approval from both your manager and the End User Computing
lead, and has a delivery time of up to 20 business days.

## Refresh cycle

Laptops are replaced every **4 years**. You will be contacted automatically about 30 days
before your laptop reaches its refresh date. You do not need to raise a request for a
scheduled refresh.

## How to request a laptop

1. Open the Service Desk portal at `https://help.halcyon-dynamics.example`.
2. Choose **Hardware > Laptop Request**.
3. Select the profile and give a business justification (required for Power and Mac).
4. Submit. The request is routed to your manager for approval.
5. After approval, standard models are delivered within **5 business days**.

## Broken, lost or stolen laptops

- **Broken:** raise a ticket under **Hardware > Laptop Fault**. If repair will take more than
  2 business days, a loan laptop is provided.
- **Lost or stolen:** report it immediately by phone on extension 4357 so the device can be
  remotely locked and wiped. Then file a report in the portal under
  **Security > Lost or Stolen Device**. Lost devices are treated as a security incident.

## Returning a laptop

When you receive a replacement, return the old laptop within **10 business days**. Do not
wipe it yourself; IT performs a certified data wipe. See KB-0010 for leaver returns.
