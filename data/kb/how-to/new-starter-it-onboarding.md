# IT Onboarding Checklist for New Starters

Welcome to Halcyon Dynamics. This checklist covers what IT sets up for you and what you need
to do in your first week.

## What happens before your start date

Managers must submit a **New Starter Request** in the Service Desk portal at least
**10 business days** before the start date. This triggers:

- Creation of your Halcyon ID account and mailbox.
- Assignment of a standard laptop (see KB-0004), shipped to your home or available at your
  office on day one.
- Group memberships and application access based on your role and department.

## Your first day

1. Sign in to the laptop with the username and temporary password from your manager's
   welcome email.
2. Change your password when prompted (minimum 14 characters, see KB-0002).
3. Enrol in MFA. You must do this within **7 days** (see KB-0003).
4. Open Outlook and Teams and confirm they load.
5. Connect to the VPN once if you are working remotely (see KB-0001).

## Your first week

- Complete the mandatory **Security Awareness** training in the learning portal within
  **14 days**. Access to sensitive systems is withheld until it is done.
- Read and accept the Acceptable Use Policy (POL-0001).
- Install any role-specific software from the Company Portal (see KB-0008).
- Request access to shared drives your team uses (see KB-0011).
- Register your building badge on a printer if you will print in the office (see KB-0006).

## Who to contact

- Missing laptop or account on day one: call the Service Desk on extension 4357. These are
  handled as **P3** with priority routing for new starters.
- Access to a specific application: raise an **Access Request** in the portal; your manager
  approves it.
