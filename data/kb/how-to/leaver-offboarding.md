# IT Offboarding for Leavers

When someone leaves Halcyon Dynamics, their access must be removed promptly and company
equipment returned. Managers are responsible for starting the process.

## Manager responsibilities

1. Submit a **Leaver Request** in the Service Desk portal as soon as the leaving date is known,
   and no later than **5 business days** before the last working day.
2. Decide what happens to the leaver's mailbox and OneDrive files: transfer to the manager,
   transfer to a colleague, or delete.
3. Arrange the handover of any shared mailboxes, distribution lists or applications the leaver
   owns.

## What IT does

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Last working day, 17:00 local time | Halcyon ID disabled; VPN and all application sign-ins blocked |
| Last working day | Mobile devices with company data are selectively wiped |
| Day 1 after leaving | Mailbox converted to a shared mailbox, if requested, and access given to the manager |
| Day 30 after leaving | Mailbox and OneDrive retained, then deleted at day 90 unless on legal hold |

For **immediate terminations**, the manager or HR calls the Service Desk on extension 4357 and
the account is disabled within **1 hour**.

## Returning equipment

- Laptops, monitors, docking stations, security keys and phones must be returned within
  **10 business days** of leaving.
- Remote leavers receive a prepaid return box. Request it in the Leaver Request form.
- Do not wipe the laptop yourself. IT performs a certified data wipe.

## Leavers keeping personal data

Leavers may copy personal, non-company files (for example personal photos) before their last
day. Copying company data to personal storage is prohibited under the Acceptable Use Policy
(POL-0001) and may be monitored.
