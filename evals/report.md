## How to read this

- **hit@k**: share of answerable questions with a chunk from an expected document in the top k results. **MRR**: average of 1/rank of the first such chunk (1.0 = always first).
- **Grounded**: the judge found every claim of the answer in the sources it cited. **Correct**: it has the key facts of the hand-written reference answer.
- **Task completion**: the agent called the expected tools, proposed the expected change (never approved, so nothing was written) and its reply said what it should.
- Small dataset (52 cases): one case moves a rate by several percentage points. The judge is an LLM (see the limitations in docs/learning-log/phase-8.md).
