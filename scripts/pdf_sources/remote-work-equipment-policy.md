# Remote Working Equipment Policy

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to employees whose contract allows them to work from home for some or all of the week. It covers the IT equipment provided by Halcyon Dynamics and the employee's responsibilities for it.

## 2. Standard home equipment

Eligible employees may request the following equipment through the Service Desk portal under Hardware > Home Equipment:

- One 27-inch monitor and one USB-C docking station.
- One wired keyboard and mouse.
- One noise-cancelling headset certified for Teams.

Additional or non-standard items, such as a second monitor or an ergonomic chair, require a workplace assessment and manager approval. Home equipment is delivered within 10 business days of approval.

## 3. Home network requirements

- A home broadband connection of at least 20 Mbps download and 5 Mbps upload is recommended for video calls.
- Home routers must use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption and must not use the default administrator password.
- All access to internal systems from home must go through the SecureLink VPN (see KB-0001).
- Halcyon Dynamics does not reimburse home broadband costs.

## 4. Working in public places

- Use a privacy screen filter when working in public places such as trains and cafes. Filters can be requested from the Service Desk.
- Do not take calls about Confidential or Restricted matters where you can be overheard.
- Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN connected.

## 5. Care of equipment

- Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of company equipment at home.
- Damage, loss or theft must be reported to the Service Desk within 24 hours. Theft must also be reported to the police and the crime reference number added to the ticket.
- Equipment must not be used by family members or other people.

## 6. Working abroad

Working from another country for more than 10 working days in a calendar year requires prior approval from HR and Information Security, because some countries restrict the use of encryption and VPN software. Laptops must not be taken to countries on the restricted list published on the intranet.

## 7. Returning equipment

All home equipment remains company property and must be returned within 10 business days of leaving the company or of the employee's role no longer being eligible for remote work.
