# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out the acceptable use of Halcyon Dynamics information systems, devices, networks and data. It applies to all employees, contractors, interns and temporary staff ("users") and to all company-owned or company-managed devices and accounts. All users must accept this policy when they join and every 12 months afterwards.

## 2. General principles

- Company systems are provided for business purposes. Limited, reasonable personal use is allowed provided it does not interfere with work, consume excessive resources, or break any other rule in this policy.
- Users are responsible for all activity performed with their Halcyon ID account.
- Users must never share their password, MFA device or Temporary Access Pass with anyone, including IT staff. IT staff will never ask for your password.

## 3. Devices

- Only company-managed devices may connect to the VPN, the Halcyon-Corp Wi-Fi network or internal systems such as shared drives and the HR Portal.
- Personal phones and tablets may access email and Teams only through the Outlook and Teams mobile apps, and only after registering on Halcyon-BYOD.
- Users must not disable, uninstall or tamper with security software, including endpoint protection, the device management agent and the SecureLink VPN client.
- Laptops must be locked (Windows + L, or Control + Command + Q on macOS) whenever left unattended, and must never be left visible in a parked car.

## 4. Data handling

- Company data must be stored only in approved locations: OneDrive for Business, SharePoint, Teams, and departmental shared drives.
- Users must not upload company data to personal cloud storage, personal email accounts or unapproved file-sharing or AI services.
- Confidential and Restricted data must be handled according to the Information Security Policy (POL-0005).
- USB storage devices are blocked by default. Exceptions require Security team approval and an encrypted company-issued drive.

## 5. Use of generative AI tools

- Only AI tools approved by the Security team and listed in the Company Portal may be used with company data.
- Confidential or Restricted data, customer personal data and source code must never be entered into public AI chatbots.
- Users remain responsible for checking the accuracy of AI-generated content before relying on it or sharing it.

## 6. Email and internet

- Users must not send chain letters, harassing or offensive content, or mass unsolicited email.
- Suspicious emails must be reported with the Report Phishing button in Outlook and must not be forwarded to colleagues.
- Access to websites in high-risk categories (for example gambling, malware and adult content) is blocked by web filtering.

## 7. Monitoring

Halcyon Dynamics monitors the use of its systems, networks and devices to protect security and to comply with legal obligations. Monitoring is carried out in line with applicable privacy law and is overseen by the Data Protection Officer. Users should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems, except where the law provides otherwise.

## 8. Breaches of this policy

Breaches may lead to suspension of access and disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal, and may be reported to law enforcement where the law has been broken. Suspected breaches should be reported to the Service Desk on extension 4357 or through the portal under Security > Report an Incident.
